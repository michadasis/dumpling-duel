How to play

The basics

  • You stand on one side of the living room, your opponent on the other. Throw the dumpling to them and catch theirs.
  • Hold to charge a throw, release to let go. The ring around the crosshair shows the power.
  • Press catch just before it reaches you. Your hands stay out for half a second.
  • Missed or dropped? Whoever is closer to it has to walk over and pick it up.
  • You can move and duck to dodge. During a free shot you can't move at all.

Classic

  • No score pressure and no penalties. Pots, bottles, a rubber duck and other random stuff sit behind each of you and make noise when hit.

Penalty

  • If a catchable throw reaches you and you don't catch it, your opponent gets a free shot at your head. Dodging a catchable throw counts as a drop.
  • During the free shot, the target can only brace or close their eyes. A headshot scores a point.
  • A throw that was never catchable is just a miss, with no penalty.

Hot Potato

  • A random player starts with it. It explodes after 40 seconds.
  • If it's in your hands, or lying near you because you didn't pick it up, you lose the round.
  • If it's between the two blue tape lines in the middle when it pops, the round is a draw.
